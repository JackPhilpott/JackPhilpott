# Jack Philpott

Humanitarian researcher and GIS/data specialist. Seven years with REACH/IMPACT Initiatives,
ICRC and FAO on needs assessments, geospatial analysis and the data systems behind them,
in Nigeria, Ukraine, South Sudan and on global programmes.

Currently Senior Research Officer with IMPACT Initiatives (REACH), leading multi-sector
needs assessments in northern Nigeria.

**Website:** [jackphilpott.github.io](https://jackphilpott.github.io), with my CV, selected work and case studies.

**What I work on**
- Survey design, sampling and data quality for large humanitarian assessments
- Automated cleaning, validation and monitoring pipelines in R and Python
- GIS and remote sensing: ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Google Earth Engine
- Dashboards and data products: R Shiny, Power BI

**Featured projects**
- [Portfolio website](https://jackphilpott.github.io)
  ([source](https://github.com/JackPhilpott/JackPhilpott.github.io)): my CV, a gallery of maps,
  dashboards and reports, and case studies. Built with Quarto.
- [msna-pipeline](https://github.com/JackPhilpott/msna-pipeline): the data pipeline I built for the
  2026 northern Nigeria MSNA, a 32,000-household survey across 11 states, rebuilt on synthetic and
  open data. Stage repos: [msna-sampling](https://github.com/JackPhilpott/msna-sampling) (two-stage
  cluster sampling and resampling, in R and Python) and
  [msna-monitoring](https://github.com/JackPhilpott/msna-monitoring) (monitoring, cleaning and
  validity checks), with a [live Shiny dashboard](https://jackphilpott.shinyapps.io/msna-monitoring/).
- [south-sudan-aok-pipeline](https://github.com/JackPhilpott/south-sudan-aok-pipeline): the R
  pipeline I ran from 2020 to 2022 for REACH's Area of Knowledge assessment in South Sudan
  (code as of 2020, no assessment data).
- [methane-s5p-explorer](https://github.com/JackPhilpott/methane-s5p-explorer) *(in progress)*:
  Sentinel-5P methane with the Earth Engine Python API. Tests, Docker and CI are in place; the
  first Earth Engine run is pending.
