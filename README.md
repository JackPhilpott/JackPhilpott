# Jack Philpott

Humanitarian researcher and GIS/data specialist. Seven years with REACH/IMPACT Initiatives,
ICRC and FAO on needs assessments, geospatial analysis and the data systems behind them,
in Nigeria, Ukraine, South Sudan and on global programmes.

Currently Senior Research Officer with IMPACT Initiatives (REACH), leading multi-sector
needs assessments in northern Nigeria.

**What I work on**
- Survey design, sampling and data quality for large humanitarian assessments
- Automated cleaning, validation and monitoring pipelines in R and Python
- GIS and remote sensing: ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Google Earth Engine
- Dashboards and data products: R Shiny, Power BI

**Featured projects**

*Household surveys and data pipelines.* Public versions of the tools I built for the 2026
northern Nigeria MSNA, a 32,000-household survey across 11 states, rebuilt on synthetic and open data:
- [msna-pipeline](https://github.com/JackPhilpott/msna-pipeline): the hub, showing how sampling,
  monitoring, cleaning, analysis and reporting fit together
- [msna-sampling](https://github.com/JackPhilpott/msna-sampling): two-stage cluster sampling, and
  resampling when areas become unreachable, in R and Python
- [msna-monitoring](https://github.com/JackPhilpott/msna-monitoring): field monitoring, cleaning
  with planted errors, validity checks and a
  [live Shiny dashboard](https://jackphilpott.shinyapps.io/msna-monitoring/)

*Displacement and mobility.*
- [south-sudan-aok-pipeline](https://github.com/JackPhilpott/south-sudan-aok-pipeline): the R
  pipeline I ran from 2020 to 2022 for REACH's Area of Knowledge assessment in South Sudan:
  monthly key-informant data on hard-to-reach settlements (code as of 2020, no assessment data)
